Privacy Policy for Quick Bite

Last Updated: August 12, 2026

Welcome to Quick Bite. This Privacy Policy explains how Quick Bite collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our mobile application and related services.

By using Quick Bite, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Quick Bite, we may collect information that you provide directly or information that is generated when you use the application.

1.1 Account Information

When you create an account or sign in to Quick Bite, we may collect information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Password authentication information
  • Profile information associated with your account
  • Information provided through supported social sign-in providers

1.2 Facebook Login

Quick Bite allows users to sign in using Facebook Login. When you choose to sign in with Facebook, Facebook may provide information permitted by the authentication process, such as your name, email address, and Facebook account identifier.

We use this information to authenticate your account and provide account-related functionality within Quick Bite.

Your use of Facebook Login is also subject to Facebook's applicable terms and privacy policies.

1.3 Google Sign-In

Quick Bite also supports Google Sign-In. When you sign in using Google, Google may provide information such as your name, email address, profile information, and Google account identifier, depending on the permissions granted.

This information is used to authenticate you and provide your Quick Bite account.

1.4 Location Information

Quick Bite may request access to your device's location to provide location-related features, such as helping you select or determine a delivery address.

Location access is requested only when required by a feature that uses location services. You can control location permissions through your device settings.

1.5 Address Information

If you provide a delivery address, we may store information such as your selected address, location details, and related delivery information so that we can process and manage your orders.

1.6 Order and Transaction Information

When you place an order through Quick Bite, we may collect and store information necessary to process and manage the order, including:

  • Ordered food items
  • Order quantities
  • Order totals
  • Delivery address
  • Payment method information or payment-related details
  • Order status
  • Order history

1.7 Payment Information

Quick Bite may provide different payment options. Where payment processing is handled by a third-party payment provider, payment information may be processed according to that provider's privacy policy and terms.

Quick Bite does not intentionally store complete payment card information unless such storage is required and securely supported by the applicable payment service.

1.8 Device and Technical Information

Certain technical information may be automatically collected when you use the application, such as device-related information, application information, diagnostic information, and technical identifiers necessary for authentication, security, and service operation.

2. How We Use Information

We may use collected information for purposes including:

  • Creating and managing your account
  • Authenticating users
  • Processing and managing food orders
  • Managing delivery addresses
  • Providing location-based functionality
  • Managing favorites and saved preferences
  • Processing payments through supported payment services
  • Providing customer support
  • Sending important account or order-related notifications
  • Improving application functionality
  • Maintaining application security
  • Detecting and preventing fraudulent or unauthorized activity
  • Complying with applicable legal requirements

3. Firebase Services

Quick Bite uses Firebase services to support certain application functionality, including authentication and data storage.

Firebase may process information on our behalf in accordance with its applicable terms and privacy practices.

Firebase services used by Quick Bite may include Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share or provide access to information when necessary to:

  • Provide authentication services
  • Process and fulfill orders
  • Provide delivery services
  • Process payments through third-party payment providers
