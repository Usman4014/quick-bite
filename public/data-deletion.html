Quick Bite - Data Deletion Instructions

Last Updated: August 12, 2026

Quick Bite respects your right to request deletion of your account and personal information associated with the application.

How to Request Account and Data Deletion

  1. Send a request to the Quick Bite support team using the contact information provided inside the Quick Bite application.
  2. Include the email address associated with your Quick Bite account.
  3. State clearly that you want your Quick Bite account and associated personal information deleted.
  4. We may ask for reasonable information needed to verify that the request belongs to the account owner.

What May Be Deleted

Subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements, we may delete or remove information associated with your account, such as account profile information and other personal information that is no longer required for a lawful purpose.

Information That May Be Retained

Some information may need to be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, accounting, dispute-resolution, or other legitimate purposes. Where retention is required, the information will be retained only as reasonably necessary for that purpose.

Facebook Login

If you created or accessed your Quick Bite account using Facebook Login, requesting deletion of your Quick Bite account will also initiate deletion of the Quick Bite information associated with that account. Facebook may separately retain information according to Facebook's own policies.

Processing Time

We will review and process valid deletion requests within a reasonable period, subject to verification and any applicable legal or operational requirements.

Important: Account deletion is different from simply uninstalling the Quick Bite application. If you want your account and associated data deleted, please submit a deletion request through the Quick Bite support contact.

Contact

For account or data deletion requests, please use the Quick Bite support contact information provided in the application.
